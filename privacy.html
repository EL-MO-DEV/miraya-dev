Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Miraya.dev is a small personal tool used by the creator of the @miraya.dev channel to upload the short Arabic tech-explainer videos they make to their own TikTok account. This page explains what data the tool uses.

What we access

When you connect a TikTok account, TikTok asks you to approve these permissions:

  • user.info.basic: your TikTok display name and avatar, shown so you can see which account is connected.
  • video.upload: to send a video you chose to your TikTok inbox as a draft. Nothing is posted until you open the draft in the TikTok app and post it yourself.

How the data is stored

  • The TikTok access token is stored only on the creator's own computer, in the tool's local settings file.
  • We do not run servers that collect your data, and we use no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Videos are uploaded only when you click the upload button, and only to the account you connected.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data. Data is sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official API, to perform the upload you asked for.

Your control

  • You can disconnect at any time from the tool, which deletes the stored token.
  • You can also remove the app's access in TikTok: Settings, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: message @miraya.dev on Instagram or TikTok.
